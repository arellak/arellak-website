Resume

I'm a software engineer with a passion for programming. I started my developer journey when I was 13 and never stopped learning since. As such, I have worked in a lot of different areas: low-level programming, mobile & desktop development, server management and game development. I have found myself to always return to web development though, which I have been doing professionally since 2023. I am also a huge Linux fan and have been using it non-stop on servers and desktops for 4 years now (mostly the Debian universe).

Professionally I have mostly worked in e-commerce. At Eshop Guide I built and customized Shopify stores for German companies and integrated Shopify apps into them. At Backwinkel I developed and maintained an OXID eShop with PHP and Elasticsearch. After that I worked on a credentialing platform for healthcare providers at Netmountains.

Since 2025 I work as a freelance developer. I work directly with clients and turn their requirements into working websites and integrations. In my own products I have integrated Stripe, Google and Microsoft calendars, SAML single sign-on and the ElevenLabs API, for which I also built an API wrapper even before they had an official one. I particularly enjoy working on complex problems where thoughtful design and engineering can lead to easy-to-use software for everyone.

My non-technical hobbies include playing the e-guitar, learning languages (e.g. Norwegian, but I am generally into linguistics, especially obscure languages like Latin or Middle English), reading (mostly mythology and a bit of philosophy) and going to the gym.
